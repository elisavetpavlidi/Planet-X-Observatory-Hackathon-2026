# Third-party data and acknowledgements

The bundled reference sky derives from [D3-Celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), copyright Olaf Frohn, under BSD-3-Clause. Its complete license is retained in `web/catalog/LICENSE`. Original downloaded data and a compact rendering catalogue remain together. This catalogue is a J2000 position reference, not dated NASA imagery.

Scientific images are actual WISE/NEOWISE or SPHEREx products, with source URLs/hashes in the data manifests and session records when supplied or obtained. The original two user-supplied M31 cutouts lack their exact original URLs; these are labelled honestly. Do not infer a download URL from an observation ID without rediscovery.

For publication/submission, follow the acknowledgement and citation requirements linked in the official [SPHEREx release overview](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/overview_qr.html) (QR2 DOI 10.26131/IRSA652; QR3 DOI 10.26131/IRSA662) and [WISE archive](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/wise.html). NASA/IPAC provides the scientific products; IMCCE supplies catalogue preselection and NASA/JPL Horizons provides ephemerides. Their availability and coverage are independent of this application. The app is not an official NASA service.
